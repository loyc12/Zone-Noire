## Recommended Approach

Preserve the full handoff, then distribute its useful material among a modest set of topical owners. Keep the current folder map and status vocabulary. Separate the approval of the filing work from the selection of fictional content, so importing an idea does not approve it.

Use 1983–1999 as the provisional working window, with greatest initial detail in 1983–1987. Retain 2000–2023 in deferred author-facing scenario notes. The author's eventual focus may extend into 2000–2003, but that does not presently give those years greater authority. The year 2000 is an organisational boundary, not an established historical turning point.

Do not work backwards from a mandatory democratic, industrialised 2023. Later possibilities should identify the earlier conditions they depend on. Early setting development may invalidate their dates, mechanisms, or outcomes.

## Task 2. Establish Approval Groups and Record Decisions

**Deliverable:** a short review sheet linking to topical owners and separating foundation candidates, working directions, and unresolved proposals.

Use the existing statuses rather than introducing a competing canon system:

| Intended distinction | Treatment before author selection | Treatment after explicit selection |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Existing foundation | Preserve the current premise and realism constraint. | Keep `Current foundation` within their existing scope. |
| Proposed new foundation | `Provisional`, identified as a foundation candidate. | `Selected baseline`, with the selected scope and decision evidence. |
| Mostly accepted direction reported by the handoff | Preserve that reported confidence, with approval still unconfirmed. Use `Unassessed` until reviewed. | `Current direction` for an author-selected but revisable direction. |
| Open mechanism, name, figure, or alternative | `Exploring` for alternatives, or `Provisional` for a specific proposal. | Promote only the selected part. |
| Later scenario | `Provisional` plus `Development: Explicitly deferred`. | Keep deferred unless its development is separately resumed. |

Foundation is a role in the setting, not proof of certainty about every attached detail. Approval, research support, and development priority remain separate. A factual claim lacking a source does not become verified when the author selects the fictional scenario around it.

### Proposed foundation candidates

Review these as individual choices or an explicitly bounded group:

- A September 1983 nuclear-war divergence associated with the Soviet false alarm. The exact escalation chain remains open
- Major destruction of Montréal and the loss of effective Canadian federal coordination
- Québec City's survival and sufficient provincial administrative continuity to sustain organised society. Whether an intended strike fails can be selected separately from its mechanism and exact target
- State survival followed by coercive, increasingly authoritarian transformation under material scarcity
- Hydro-Québec as a central reconstruction institution, with food, water, energy, transport, and technical capacity strongly shaping political power

These are recommendations for review, not statements that the author has already selected them.

### Proposed working directions

The handoff repeatedly supports Lévesque's initial leadership, severe famine and public-health collapse, gradual administrative sovereignty, refugee pressure, border militarisation, coercive labour institutions, security reorganisation, electrical islanding, petroleum scarcity, rail reconstruction, and surviving technical knowledge. These are good candidates for `Current direction` if endorsed.

Separate each broad direction from its contingent details. For example, accepting Hydro's enlarged role does not select “le Corps”, an organisational chart, or railway electrification dates. Accepting an authoritarian trajectory does not imply that every proposed abuse was necessary or inevitable.

### Details to leave open

Keep exact targets, failure mechanisms, losses, migration volumes, succession arrangements, camp rules, currency, formal names, Indigenous political settlements, neighbouring states, and recovery schedules unresolved. Lévesque's proposed death around 1987 and its political consequences require distinct selection. All 2000–2023 outcomes remain deferred proposals, including items described as stable in section 44.

**Completion check:** each imported group has an intelligible status, and no source confidence claim has been silently converted into author approval. Record decisions at the owning note or a linked decision entry, without duplicating the detailed answer in the review sheet.

## Task 6. Expose Dependencies and Prepare the Research Queue

**Deliverable:** a short prioritised queue linked to questions in their owning notes. This task identifies research work, without undertaking a full historical or technical audit during intake.

The first issues worth surfacing are:

- **Divergence and escalation:** distinguish the selected departure from history from the still-unverified chain leading to the exchange
- **Québec City and Valcartier:** surviving civilian government and usable military capacity depend on related but separable target and damage assumptions
- **Climate and famine duration:** the handoff describes a shock diminishing over several years, climatic normalisation in its late-1990s–2000s arc, and scarcity easing in the 1990s. These need reconciliation. Separate climate recovery from food-system and institutional recovery
- **Demographic timing:** “demographic low point” in winter 1984–1985 is ambiguous alongside prolonged famine. Clarify whether this means peak mortality, worst living conditions, or minimum population
- **Lévesque and succession:** a real-world death date does not establish the alternate-history date, and the link between his death and the end of democratic continuity remains a proposed causal choice
- **Hydro and the North:** surviving generation does not itself establish transmission, maintenance, resupply, or political access. Indigenous relations affect the early setting, even if a final settlement remains open
- **Refugees, food, and coercion:** preserve proposed discriminatory outcomes as contingent policies and social processes. Do not present exclusion or forced labour as scientifically proven necessities
- **Recovery:** food surplus, trade, skilled labour, equipment, and institutional choices constrain the timing. Preserve their dependencies without allowing the proposed 2023 outcome to decide them in advance

Suggested research order once authorised: 1983 institutional and infrastructure baseline, exchange assumptions, first-year survival constraints, then the development of emergency institutions and regional differences. Ask focused questions before commissioning broad research.

**Completion check:** consequential tensions have owners, unresolved inputs remain visible, and research findings are kept distinct from fictional selections.
