# Québec City and Valcartier

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current direction**

**Source: Revised handoff §§4, 20, imported under [[00 Core/03 Integration Decisions|the batch-two decision]]**

Québec City survives an intended strike because the attack fails. This foundational choice does not determine the failure mechanism or usable assets at Valcartier. The city becomes the surviving political and administrative centre.

## Québec City's Survival

Québec City's survival is foundational.

The city itself is targeted or endangered, but one or more intended Soviet warheads fail to function properly.

Possible mechanisms include:

- missile failure
- guidance failure
- warhead failure
- launch failure
- interception is less desirable unless independently justified
- target-assignment error

The exact mechanism remains **OPEN**.

The survival later acquires symbolic importance.

Possible later term:

- **"Le Miracle de Québec"** — PROVISIONAL

This should not necessarily imply a religious consensus.

It may function as:

- a popular phrase
- state propaganda
- dark humour
- patriotic mythology
- religious interpretation for some groups
- shorthand for the city's statistical luck

Québec City thereafter becomes the main surviving political and administrative centre of Québec.

## Valcartier

Valcartier is strategically important.

It provides:

- trained military personnel
- command structure
- vehicles
- logistics
- engineering capacity
- communications
- weapons
- institutional continuity

However, it may itself plausibly have been a Soviet target.

This creates a useful possibility:

The failed strike associated with Québec City may have been intended for:

- Québec City proper
- Valcartier
- the Québec City–Valcartier strategic region
- multiple targets in the region

The exact targeting model remains OPEN.
