# Québec City and Valcartier

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current direction**

**Source: Revised handoff §§4, 20, imported under [[00 Core/03 Integration Decisions|the batch-two decision]]**

Québec City survives intended Soviet attacks because the weapons aimed at it fail to detonate or miss significantly. Failure to detonate is the preferred possibility, not a selected mechanism. This foundational choice does not determine the number of weapons, their failure modes or usable assets at Valcartier. The city becomes the surviving political and administrative centre.

## Québec City's Survival

Québec City's survival is foundational.

The city itself is targeted or endangered, but the intended strikes fail to destroy it.

The preferred mechanisms to assess are:

- guidance failure
- warhead failure

The handoff also proposed broader missile failure, launch failure, interception and target-assignment error. They remain source alternatives for review, but do not by themselves explain the author's current account of weapons directed at the city failing to detonate or missing significantly.

The exact mechanism remains **OPEN**.

Wildfires reaching the city's outskirts are a possibility, not a selected outcome. Their plausibility depends on nearby targets, ignition and fire spread and requires research.

The survival later acquires symbolic importance.

Possible later term:

- **"Le Miracle de Québec"** — PROVISIONAL

This should not necessarily imply a religious consensus.

It may function as:

- a popular phrase
- state propaganda
- dark humour
- patriotic mythology
- religious interpretation for some groups
- shorthand for the city's statistical luck

Québec City thereafter becomes the main surviving political and administrative centre of Québec.

## Valcartier

Valcartier is strategically important.

It provides:

- trained military personnel
- command structure
- vehicles
- logistics
- engineering capacity
- communications
- weapons
- institutional continuity

However, it may itself plausibly have been a Soviet target.

This creates a useful possibility:

The failed strike associated with Québec City may have been intended for:

- Québec City proper
- Valcartier
- the Québec City–Valcartier strategic region
- multiple targets in the region

The exact targeting model remains OPEN.
