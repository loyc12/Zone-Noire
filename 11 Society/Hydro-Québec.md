# Hydro-Québec

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Current direction**

**Source: Revised handoff §§7 and §41C, imported under [[00 Core/03 Integration Decisions|the batch-two decision]]**

Hydro-Québec becomes a reconstruction institution with substantial political influence. Its evolving functions below are the working direction, not a fixed organisational chart or a guarantee that every capability exists immediately.

## Hydro-Québec as a Reconstruction Institution

Hydro-Québec is one of the central institutions of the setting.

Its early reconstruction role does not establish access to northern generation. The author suspects northern plants or transmission could be lost to the southern system for years. [[99 Workshop/Research/1983 Québec Electricity Baseline|The electricity source scan]] offers provisional service tiers and targeted next research; neither the plant data nor those options establish Hydro's early power supply or northern reach.

Rather than simply continuing as an electrical utility, it gradually becomes a quasi-state reconstruction apparatus.

This evolves organically because it already possesses:

- engineers
- technicians
- logistics capacity
- telecommunications
- heavy equipment
- regional infrastructure
- hydroelectric generating assets
- technical management culture
- province-wide organisational reach

Hydro's mandate progressively expands into:

- electrical generation
- grid stabilisation
- transmission repair
- telecommunications
- municipal electrical restoration
- water pumping
- sanitation infrastructure
- industrial energy allocation
- railway electrification
- heavy civil engineering
- machine-shop operations
- equipment salvage
- spare-parts fabrication
- dam maintenance
- strategic industrial planning

Over time Hydro effectively becomes part:

- utility
- reconstruction ministry
- civil engineering corps
- industrial conglomerate
- logistical institution
- emergency infrastructure command

Possible colloquial term:

- **"le Corps"** — PROVISIONAL

Possible more formal future titles remain OPEN.

Hydro eventually becomes one of the three major postwar power centres:

1. civilian political administration
2. military/security institutions
3. Hydro-Québec / technical reconstruction apparatus

Hydro accumulates extensive **soft power** rather than necessarily formally governing.
