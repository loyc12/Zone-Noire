# External Relations and Trade

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Exploring**

**Source: Revised handoff §§38 and §41L, imported under [[00 Core/03 Integration Decisions|the batch-two decision]]**

Continental central governments lose coherent control during the immediate crisis. Surviving institutions and potential trading partners must still be distinguished from selected successor states. Early cross-border conditions belong here, while specific post-2000 trade outcomes remain deferred.

## Foreign Relations

OPEN DEVELOPMENT AREA.

Potential neighbouring political entities:

- remnants or successor governments in Ontario
- Maritime successor states
- Newfoundland
- New England polities
- surviving American federal authorities
- regional American states
- Prairie governments
- Indigenous governments
- European or Atlantic trading partners

Québec's eventual reconstruction depends partly on when meaningful external trade resumes.
