## Reading the Statuses

- `Provisional` marks a specific proposal awaiting selection, including the foundation candidates below
- `Unassessed` preserves the handoff's reported confidence without claiming independent author endorsement
- `Exploring` marks unresolved alternatives
- `Selected baseline` becomes appropriate only for an explicitly selected, bounded setting decision
- `Current direction` becomes appropriate only for an author-selected but revisable direction
- `Development: Explicitly deferred` applies to all material from 2000 onward, independently of its source wording

The rows below are proposed review units, not an exhaustive substitute for the source. Approving a broad concept would leave the stated details open. Historical and technical assertions remain unverified during this batch, even if a surrounding fictional scenario is later selected.

## Issues Requiring Review

- **R1 — Climate and scarcity:** distinguish several years of climatic disruption from the later normalisation and prolonged food scarcity described in §§2, 11 and 40
- **R2 — Demographic timing:** clarify whether the winter 1984–1985 “demographic low point” means peak mortality, worst conditions or minimum population (§40)
- **R3 — Political timing:** separate Lévesque's proposed death around 1987 from the date and mechanism of authoritarian consolidation (§§6, 29, 40)
- **R4 — Survival dependencies:** separate Québec City survival, military capacity, northern power generation, grid access and political agreements (§§4, 7–8, 20, 23)
- **R5 — Policy and recovery claims:** preserve coercive policies and eventual recovery as contingent proposals rather than demonstrated necessities or assured outcomes (§§14–18, 26, 28–31, 39, 42)

These are review flags, not resolutions or completed research findings.
