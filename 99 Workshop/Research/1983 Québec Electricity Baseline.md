# 1983 Québec Electricity Baseline

**Type: Research note**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Pre-event generation sources and unselected early-service options**

## Question and inputs

Which sources can establish the generating plant, output and demand baseline around September 1983? Later service estimates also need transmission routes, controls, damage and repair capacity. Selected service patterns belong in [[10 Technologies and Infrastructure/Electricity and Communications|Electricity and Communications]]; Hydro's institutional role belongs in [[11 Society/Hydro-Québec|Hydro-Québec]].

## Evidence

- [Statistics Canada, Electric Power Statistics, volume III](https://publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.827389/publication.html), **1983 inventory as of 31 December 1983**, published in 1984: lists Canadian generating plants by owner, location, installation dates, generator counts and capacity. The catalogue offers the 1983 scan. A December list may include equipment commissioned after the September divergence, so each plant needs a commissioning-date check
- [Statistics Canada, Electric Power Statistics, volume II](https://publications.gc.ca/site/fra/9.827236/publication.html), 1983 annual data: records electricity supply and disposition, utility finances and thermal-station fuel consumption. Annual output cannot be read as the amount available after a September catastrophe
- [Statistics Canada, Electric Power Statistics monthly series](https://www.publications.gc.ca/site/eng/9.826452/publication.html), 1983 issues including August and September: potential near-event generation and disposition baseline, with provincial and utility categories to verify in the actual issue
- [Statistics Canada, Electric Power Statistics, volume I](https://publications.gc.ca/site/fra/9.827269/publication.html), 1983 capability and load series: source lead for available capacity, peak demand and reserve rather than installed nameplate capacity alone; confirm the catalogue item and table definitions before extracting values
- [Hydro-Québec corporate chronology](https://www.hydroquebec.com/investor-relations/why-invest.html), modern retrospective: lists major plant commissioning by year, including Gentilly-2 in 1983. It does not establish which 1983 units were operating by late September

## Analysis and uncertainty

Installed capacity, generation over a year, peak capacity and electricity deliverable at a particular town are separate quantities. Northern generation might physically survive while the southern grid, control system, lines, crews or spare parts do not. This quick pass establishes source locations; it does not yet support a 1983 capacity total or a post-event power map.

## Choices and outcome

**Gap:** The source list points to plant and load records, but their figures have not been extracted. More importantly, those records cannot directly measure electricity deliverable after the fictional damage. Exact line failures, controls, crews and repair stocks may be inaccessible

**Recommended provisional gap-filler:** E1 models early service by **regional tiers**: isolated local supply where generation and control survive, intermittent supply where connections can be restored, and no useful service where transmission or staff are lost. Treat northern generation as physically possible but unavailable to southern Québec until a specific surviving route is established. This is a working abstraction, not a claim about which town has power

**Alternatives:** E2 assumes a near-total southern outage until a later reconstruction milestone. This simplifies the early setting but gives Hydro less local capacity; it should be tested against any surviving nearby generation and lines

**Deferred research:** Read the 1983 plant inventory, pre-event load data and a transmission map for the major corridors. Check 1983 commissioning dates, then combine these with explicit strike and line-damage assumptions. Use broad service tiers if control and repair details remain unavailable rather than inventing precise megawatts for each locality

**Authorial veto / outcome:** Pending. Choose E1, E2 or another damage-conditioned model. No northern-grid or local-service outcome is selected here
