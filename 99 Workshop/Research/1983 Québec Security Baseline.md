# 1983 Québec Security Baseline

**Type: Research note**

**Status: Exploring**

**Scope: Pre-event security organisations and unselected coordination options**

## Question and inputs

Which military and police structures existed in Québec in September 1983, and where can a later pass find period-accurate personnel, equipment and command evidence? Selected force arrangements belong in [[11 Society/Military and Security|Military and Security]].

## Evidence

- [Canadian Army, 5e Groupe-brigade history](https://www.canada.ca/fr/armee/organisation/2-division-du-canada/5-groupe-brigade-mecanise-du-canada.html), modern official retrospective: the Valcartier formation became the **5e Groupe-brigade du Canada** in 1977 and did not take the later mechanised-brigade name until 1989. Its present unit list and strength cannot be projected back to 1983
- [Department of National Defence, 5 Combat Engineer Regiment lineage](https://www.canada.ca/en/department-national-defence/services/military-history/history-heritage/official-military-history-lineages/lineages/engineer-regiments/5-combat-engineer.html), official lineage: the regular-force regiment was authorised at Valcartier in June 1977. Lineage confirms an organisation's existence, not its 1983 strength or readiness
- [Sûreté du Québec, institutional history](https://www.sq.gouv.qc.ca/organisation/histoire/), official retrospective: the force had nine districts before 1983, and its 1981–1987 hiring pause is relevant to staffing. This history dates the Côte-Nord district to 1979, while [another SQ history page](https://www.patrimoine.sq.gouv.qc.ca/?a=internetp150) gives 1977; the precise creation date needs checking if used. Neither source establishes September 1983 personnel totals
- [Sûreté du Québec, 1983 organisational charts](https://www.patrimoine.sq.gouv.qc.ca/Fiche/Organigramme-organisationnel-1983-a20230019), archival extract from its 1983 activity report, published 1984: covers headquarters and the nine districts **as of 31 December 1983**. The charts are a promising command-source lead, but their date falls after the divergence and they have not been read for unit details

## Analysis and uncertainty

The baseline has at least distinct federal military, provincial police and municipal police chains. Seek period staffing, authority and supply evidence to constrain a mixed-force scenario; exact unit rosters are unlikely to be necessary for a useful estimate. The SQ's late-1983 chart can help reconstruct actual history but is not a snapshot of this timeline after September. Valcartier's survival remains dependent on the physical-damage investigation.

## Choices and outcome

**Gap:** The sources establish organisations and a nine-district SQ structure, but not an exact September 1983 force roster, equipment stock or deployable strength. The SQ history's nearly 5,000 employees after its earlier restructuring is a rough organisational scale, not a 1983 count of available officers

**Recommended provisional gap-filler:** S1 begins with **separate military, SQ and municipal police chains that coordinate emergency tasks**, then integrates surviving personnel gradually under Québec direction. Use **a few thousand SQ employees province-wide** only as an order-of-magnitude planning proxy; distinguish employees from armed officers and subtract unknown losses before assigning field capacity. Keep Valcartier personnel contingent on local survival

**Alternatives:** S2 creates a unified command almost immediately, while retaining distinct unit identities beneath it. This fits the desired rapid reorganisation but requires stronger assumptions about communications, legal authority and surviving leaders

**Deferred research:** Read the 1983 SQ charts and seek period personnel reports, military orders of battle, RCMP and municipal records, plus emergency-law sources. If detailed composition stays inaccessible, use rounded staffing bands and mission capacity rather than exact regimental rosters

**Authorial veto / outcome:** Pending. Choose S1, S2 or another plausible sequence. No conscription timetable, headcount or final command structure is selected here
