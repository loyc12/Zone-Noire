# Research Method

**Type: Authorial guide**

**Status: Active**

Use this method for one bounded question from [[99 Workshop/Research/Research Topics|Research Topics]] at a time. Related small questions may share a pass. The [[99 Workshop/00 Revision Priorities|revision queue]] owns work order and deferrals. A research note is evidence and analysis, not setting canon.

1. Define the question, 1983–1987 scope, topical owner and any inputs needed from other topics. If an input is unknown, state the temporary assumption or range rather than waiting for unrelated research
2. Keep the work in one self-contained note in this folder. Use four short sections: **Question and inputs**, **Evidence**, **Analysis and uncertainty**, and **Choices and outcome**. Record sources and their relevant dates, coverage and limits. Distinguish real-world facts, scenario assumptions, inferences and selected fiction; show units and assumptions for estimates
3. End with what the evidence supports, what remains open and any consequential choice for the author. After an authorial selection, update the topical owner and affected dependencies, and retain the research note as provenance. Split a topic into more notes only when the material needs it

Each topic owns its supporting evidence. Link only the inputs or conclusions another topic actually needs. If later evidence conflicts with a selected premise, surface the conflict for review rather than silently changing the setting.
