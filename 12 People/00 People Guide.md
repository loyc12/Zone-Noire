# People Guide

**Type: Authorial guide**

**Status: Active**

`12 People` holds concise reference profiles for historical people and fictional figures relevant to the setting. Each page identifies the person's basis and the status of their in-setting role. Supporting research stays in `99 Workshop/Research`, while institutional details stay with their topical owner. Story-specific arcs and viewpoints belong in `80 Narrative` when needed.

## Use

1. Copy [[12 People/Person Profile Template|Person Profile Template]] to a note named for the person, then replace or remove unused sections and snipets
2. Set **Basis** to `Real`, `Inspired`, or `Fictional`. `Real` means the historical individual, including that person's place in this alternate timeline. `Inspired` means an invented person built from a real person's incomplete profile with fictional gap-fillers. `Fictional` means a person created for this setting without a specific real-person basis
3. Keep the snapshot short. **Status** applies to the in-setting role, not to the truth of historical evidence. Separate documented biography or established background from behavioural interpretation and in-setting choices. For an inspired person, mark which details come from the source person and which were invented. Cite consequential historical claims, label uncertainty, and link to the research note rather than copying its evidence here

Update an in-setting choice when the author selects it. Avoid creating subfolders until the number of profiles makes them useful.
