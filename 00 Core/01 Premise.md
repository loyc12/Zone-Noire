# Premise

**Type: Project brief**

**Status: Current foundation**

**Scope: The author's initial setting brief only**

Zone Noire is intended to explore a realistic post-nuclear-apocalypse Québec.

## Open Boundaries

The date, time elapsed since the catastrophe, extent and course of the nuclear conflict, affected places, surviving infrastructure, communities, protagonists, tone, and narrative form remain undecided.

The title does not yet designate an in-setting place, administrative boundary, or hazard classification.

Further setting development is deferred until the author resumes it. [[99 Workshop/00 Revision Priorities|Revision Priorities]] records the initial questions without selecting answers.
