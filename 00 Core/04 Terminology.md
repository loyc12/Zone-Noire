# Terminology

**Type: Setting reference**

**Status: Provisional**

**Source: Revised handoff §43, imported under [[00 Core/03 Integration Decisions|the batch-two decision]]**

All terms below remain proposals. Importing the stable setting has not approved labels, institutional titles, period names or the in-setting meaning of the project title. Definitions follow the author-corrected source. Post-2000 terminology and periodisation are explicitly deferred.

**IMPORTANT:** This entire glossary is subject to manual review. None of these labels should automatically be treated as canon.

## Le Jour

Possible shorthand for 26 September 1983, the day of the nuclear exchange.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Le Miracle de Québec

Popular or retrospective term for Québec City's survival after the intended Soviet strike fails.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## La Continuité

Possible historiographical term for the period between the nuclear exchange and René Lévesque's death, during which the emergency government still derives direct legitimacy from the prewar democratic government.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Le Gouvernement d'urgence

Possible term for the authoritarian emergency regime that consolidates after Lévesque's death.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Hydro / le Corps

Colloquial terminology for the transformed Hydro-Québec reconstruction apparatus.

"Le Corps" implies Hydro has effectively evolved into a civil engineering and infrastructure corps.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Le Réseau

Possible social and political shorthand for the restored electrical and communications network.

To be "sur le Réseau" may imply effective integration into the functioning state.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Le Grand Hiver

Possible popular term for the multiyear post-nuclear climatic shock.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## L'Été Noir

Possible name for the catastrophic 1984 growing season.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Crédits d'Établissement

Possible labour/resettlement credit system used to promise camp residents eventual permanent status.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Zone blanche

Possible radiological/agricultural classification for land considered usable with monitoring.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Zone grise

Possible classification for restricted or contaminated agricultural land.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Zone noire

Possible classification for land considered unsafe for normal permanent habitation or agriculture.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## La Milice

Possible colloquial name for the postwar Québec armed/security force.

May descend from Canadian Forces, police, auxiliaries, and conscripts.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Forces territoriales du Québec

Possible formal military title.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Ateliers d'État

Possible term for state-controlled industrial labour facilities.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Centres d'Affectation Industrielle - CDAI

Possible formal analogue to the camp labour system for industrial work.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Centres de Restoration Agricole - CDRA

Possible formal analogue to the camp labour system for agricultural work.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## Le Redressement

Possible retrospective term for the reconstruction period beginning once famine recedes and industrial growth resumes.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## La Transition

Possible term for the period in which emergency institutions are dismantled and democracy returns.

Status: **PROVISIONAL**

## La République de Québec

Possible later constitutional self-description for the restored democratic Québec state.

This assumes a republican naming convention that has **not** been established and may be unsuitable.

Status: **VERY PROVISIONAL - NAME NEEDS REVIEW**
